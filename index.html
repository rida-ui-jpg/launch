How it works

Venture investing — gamified.

Three steps to your first portfolio. No money required, just your instincts.

01
🏈

Draft your portfolio

Each season, browse hundreds of real early-stage startups and draft 10 into your portfolio — just like picking players on draft day.

02
📈

Earn points on milestones

Your startups earn points when they raise funding, ship products, get press coverage, or hit growth milestones. Real data, real scores.

03
🏆

Compete and climb

Join leagues with friends or strangers. Climb the leaderboard. End-of-season rankings reveal who actually has the eye for great startups.

Features

Built for the curious investor.

Everything you need to follow the startup world like a pro — without the $50K minimum check.

🔍

Smart startup discovery

Browse startups by sector, stage, geography, and founding team. Filter by what you actually care about — not just what's trending on TechCrunch.

⚡

Real-time milestone tracking

We monitor funding announcements, product launches, hiring signals, and press. Your score updates automatically — no manual tracking needed.

🤝

League play with friends

Create private leagues with your business school cohort, your investing club, or just your group chat. See who's been talking the talk — and who can walk it.

Public leagues also available for those who want the real competition.
